## What's new

### Continue Watching, Next Up and Latest on Emby 4.10 and later
- On Emby Server 4.10 and later (4.11 included), Home showed only My List and your libraries: Emby keeps its home screen as a new list of sections, which Emby5 didn't read, and the older layout it read instead was out of date. Emby5 now reads the new list, so Home follows the order you set on your server, with Continue Watching (and Next Up in it, when "Include next up" is on) and Latest. ([#11](https://github.com/bornaradusin/Emby5/issues/11))
- On older Emby servers and on Jellyfin, a home screen slot you never changed now shows the server's default for it, as the server's own apps do: before, Emby5 stopped reading at the first unchanged slot and could leave out Continue Watching and Latest.
- A home screen layout Emby5 can't follow now shows every row, rather than only My List.

Playback and video decoding are unchanged.

## Installation and updating

1. Close Emby5 completely.
2. Download **Emby5-1.3.6.zip** from the assets below, not a source-code archive.
3. Extract the ZIP to obtain the **PPSA99515/** folder.
4. Copy the complete folder into **/data/homebrew/**. For an update, merge and overwrite the existing application files.
5. Wait for the transfer to finish, then launch Emby5 and confirm **1.3.6** under **Settings > System > Installed version**.
