## What's new

### Shuffle episodes
- A show's page has a **Shuffle** button next to Play. It starts a random episode and plays on through the rest of the show in random order, each episode from its start. Specials (season 0) are left out, and each episode keeps its own season and episode number in the player. ([#11](https://github.com/bornaradusin/Emby5/issues/11))

### "Included in" on film pages
- A film's page can show the collections the film is in, above "More like this"; ✕ on one opens it. ([#11](https://github.com/bornaradusin/Emby5/issues/11))
- It's off by default: turn it on under **Settings › Playback › Show "Included in" on film pages**. Emby and Jellyfin can't say which collections a film is in, so the first film page opened reads your collections once in the background (a few seconds on a large server; the page doesn't wait for it). The list is kept for ten minutes, so a film added to a collection on the server shows there within ten minutes.

Playback and video decoding are unchanged.

## Installation and updating

1. Close Emby5 completely.
2. Download **Emby5-1.3.4.zip** from the assets below, not a source-code archive.
3. Extract the ZIP to obtain the **PPSA99515/** folder.
4. Copy the complete folder into **/data/homebrew/**. For an update, merge and overwrite the existing application files.
5. Wait for the transfer to finish, then launch Emby5 and confirm **1.3.4** under **Settings > System > Installed version**.
