## What's new

### Upscaling
- Two new upscalers, both small neural networks that double the picture: **Film (FSRCNNX)** for live action and **Anime (ArtCNN)** for anime and other drawn pictures. They are in **Settings › Playback › Upscaling** and on the player's upscaling button, next to **Fast (FSR 1)** (until now just "FSR 1") and **Anime4K**.
- **Auto** now goes by what is playing: **Anime** when the genres include Anime or Animation (for an episode, its series' genres), **Film** for other films and episodes, and **Fast** for Live TV. If the console can't keep up, it still steps down to Fast and then Off.
- Your upscaling choice from earlier versions is kept.
- Fixed upscaling turning off during playback with "Off (GPU scratch … retrying)" when the next video had a different size. Emby5 now sets aside the graphics memory it needs for upscaling once (106 MiB) and keeps it, instead of changing it for each video. **GPU scratch** under **L3** shows it.
- Videos larger than 1080p now play at their own size without upscaling (the player says "Off (source above 1080p)"). They are already close to the TV's 4K.

## Installation and updating

1. Close Emby5 completely.
2. Download **Emby5-1.2.11.zip** from the assets below, not a source-code archive.
3. Extract the ZIP to obtain the **PPSA99515/** folder.
4. Copy the complete folder into **/data/homebrew/**. For an update, merge and overwrite the existing application files.
5. Wait for the transfer to finish, then launch Emby5 and confirm **1.2.11** under **Settings > System > Installed version**.
