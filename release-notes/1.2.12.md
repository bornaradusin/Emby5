## What's new

### Blu-ray disc images
- Blu-ray disc images (**.iso**) on your server now play directly, with no mounting and no transcoding. Emby5 looks inside the image, finds the main film and plays it, reading only the parts it needs over the network.
- Seeking and resuming work, and the audio and subtitle tracks show their languages.
- Opening a disc image can take a little longer than a normal file, as Emby5 reads the image's contents first.
- 4K (UHD) disc images don't use the PS5's hardware video decoder yet, so they may not play smoothly.

Thanks to [whity2333](https://github.com/whity2333) for this ([#7](https://github.com/bornaradusin/Emby5/issues/7), [#8](https://github.com/bornaradusin/Emby5/pull/8)).

## Installation and updating

1. Close Emby5 completely.
2. Download **Emby5-1.2.12.zip** from the assets below, not a source-code archive.
3. Extract the ZIP to obtain the **PPSA99515/** folder.
4. Copy the complete folder into **/data/homebrew/**. For an update, merge and overwrite the existing application files.
5. Wait for the transfer to finish, then launch Emby5 and confirm **1.2.12** under **Settings > System > Installed version**.
