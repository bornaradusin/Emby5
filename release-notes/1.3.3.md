## What's fixed

### Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Thai subtitles
- Subtitles in Chinese, Japanese, Korean or Thai no longer show boxes. 1.3.2 loaded the PS5's font for those characters but the subtitle renderer never used it: it only takes a font that a subtitle's style asks for by name, or its default font. The PS5's font for the subtitle's characters is now the default font while that subtitle is shown, so a styled .ass asking for Windows fonts (such as 微软雅黑) and a plain .srt both draw their Chinese, Japanese, Korean or Thai text. English lines in a plain .srt keep the usual font. ([#10](https://github.com/bornaradusin/Emby5/issues/10))

## What's new

### Theme videos
- When a film or show has a theme video on your server, it can play behind the title's page in place of the backdrop picture, over and over while the page is open, with its sound as quiet as theme music. A title without one still plays its theme music. ([#9](https://github.com/bornaradusin/Emby5/issues/9))
- It's off for now: turn it on under **Settings › Playback › Theme videos**. Emby and Jellyfin take theme videos from the video files in a title's **backdrops** folder. A theme video in HDR plays its sound but keeps the backdrop picture, as the menus are in SDR.

Playback and video decoding are unchanged.

## Installation and updating

1. Close Emby5 completely.
2. Download **Emby5-1.3.3.zip** from the assets below, not a source-code archive.
3. Extract the ZIP to obtain the **PPSA99515/** folder.
4. Copy the complete folder into **/data/homebrew/**. For an update, merge and overwrite the existing application files.
5. Wait for the transfer to finish, then launch Emby5 and confirm **1.3.3** under **Settings > System > Installed version**.
