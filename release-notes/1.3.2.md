## What's fixed

### Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Thai subtitles
- Subtitles in Chinese, Japanese, Korean or Thai no longer show broken characters when the subtitle file has no language set, such as a fansub .ass named after the film. Emby5 now looks at the text itself and loads the PS5's own font for the characters it finds. Tracks inside the video get the same check. ([#10](https://github.com/bornaradusin/Emby5/issues/10))
- Subtitle files that aren't saved as UTF-8 show correctly. That covers the older Chinese (GBK, Big5), Japanese (Shift-JIS), Korean, Cyrillic, Arabic and other encodings, in .srt and .ass alike. The encoding is worked out from the file itself, and three-letter languages from Emby and Jellyfin (such as `chi`, `jpn` and `rus`) are now understood.

Playback and video decoding are unchanged.

## Installation and updating

1. Close Emby5 completely.
2. Download **Emby5-1.3.2.zip** from the assets below, not a source-code archive.
3. Extract the ZIP to obtain the **PPSA99515/** folder.
4. Copy the complete folder into **/data/homebrew/**. For an update, merge and overwrite the existing application files.
5. Wait for the transfer to finish, then launch Emby5 and confirm **1.3.2** under **Settings > System > Installed version**.
