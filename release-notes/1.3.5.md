## What's new

### Continue Watching and Next Up on Home
- A home screen whose "keep watching" section isn't one of the standard ones (a section that merges Continue Watching with Next Up, or one a plugin adds) showed neither row in Emby5. Emby5 now recognises such a section: it shows Continue Watching there, followed by Next Up when the layout has no separate Next Up. ([#11](https://github.com/bornaradusin/Emby5/issues/11))

### Seasons as posters
- A show's page can pick its season by poster, like Emby's own apps: each season's poster (the show's when the season has none) with its name under it. It's off by default: turn it on under **Settings › Playback › Show seasons as posters**. Moving, the Options menu and the episodes below work as with the season names. ([#11](https://github.com/bornaradusin/Emby5/issues/11))

Playback and video decoding are unchanged.

## Installation and updating

1. Close Emby5 completely.
2. Download **Emby5-1.3.5.zip** from the assets below, not a source-code archive.
3. Extract the ZIP to obtain the **PPSA99515/** folder.
4. Copy the complete folder into **/data/homebrew/**. For an update, merge and overwrite the existing application files.
5. Wait for the transfer to finish, then launch Emby5 and confirm **1.3.5** under **Settings > System > Installed version**.
