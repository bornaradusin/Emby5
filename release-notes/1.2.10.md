## What's new

### Servers
- Emby5 now connects to Emby- and Jellyfin-compatible servers that use their own name (unofficial builds and rebranded servers), instead of saying "This is not an Emby or Jellyfin server". It works out by itself whether such a server works like Emby or like Jellyfin.

## Installation and updating

1. Close Emby5 completely.
2. Download **Emby5-1.2.10.zip** from the assets below, not a source-code archive.
3. Extract the ZIP to obtain the **PPSA99515/** folder.
4. Copy the complete folder into **/data/homebrew/**. For an update, merge and overwrite the existing application files.
5. Wait for the transfer to finish, then launch Emby5 and confirm **1.2.10** under **Settings > System > Installed version**.
