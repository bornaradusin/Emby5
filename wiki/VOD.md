The **VOD** tab shows video on demand from Xtream catalogues, and single videos from M3U playlists. It appears when at least one film or series is available.

- **Movies** and **TV Shows**, each with artwork and rows per category. A provider with only one kind shows only that one.
- A film's page has its artwork, description, running time, and **Play** or **Resume**.
- An Xtream series has a season picker and its episodes, when the provider gives that information.
- **△** searches the selected type (Movies or TV Shows) in every category of the catalogue, not just the cards on screen. **○** clears or closes the search.
- Coming back from a page or from playing returns you to the same category and title.

## Resume points

VOD progress is kept on the console, separately from your Emby or Jellyfin history, for each user, account and provider.

- **Options › Continue Watching (local)** lists what you've started.
- A title's page offers **Resume**, **Play from beginning** and **Options › Remove local resume point**.

## Refreshing the catalogue

At the bottom of **Settings › Live TV**, **VOD** shows the catalogue's status and how many titles are loaded, lets you refresh by hand, and sets how often it refreshes: **Manual only, every 12 hours, 24 hours (the default), 48 hours or 7 days**.

- Films and series refresh separately, with their own counts and retries. A failed series refresh doesn't throw away the films.
- Categories are loaded one by one, and each checked batch can be used before the refresh is done. Cached titles stay usable meanwhile.
- The catalogue is cached on the console, so it isn't downloaded every time, but playing still needs the provider.

Press **○** while a stream is opening to cancel it.

## Limits

How complete a catalogue is depends on the provider: the size of its answers, its categories and the information it gives. An M3U entry often doesn't say which series, season or episode it is, so it can appear as a single title.
