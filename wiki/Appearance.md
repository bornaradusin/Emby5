Open **Settings › System › Appearance**.

## Themes

There are **32 themes**: **Emby5** (near-black backgrounds, dark glass and bright green, the default for new settings), the original blue **Glass**, and **30 more** inspired by [BlackBearReloaded's PS5 Homebrew UI](https://github.com/blackbearreloaded/ps5-homebrew-ui). They're drawn by Emby5's own renderer, so they are adaptations rather than exact copies.

An appearance you saved before stays as it is. To use the new default, choose **Emby5** and apply it. Choosing a theme resets your unsaved changes, but not your saved custom themes.

## What you can change

| Section | |
| --- | --- |
| **Themes & Colours** | Theme, matching colour variant, accent colour |
| **Buttons & Panels** | Button shape; theme default, solid or glass; normal or strong selection outline |
| **Background** | Background colour, artwork or plain, backdrop dimming |
| **Text & Browsing Layout** | Interface text at 90%, 100%, 110% or 120%; compact, default or large posters |
| **Motion & Reading** | Enlarging the selected poster, reduced motion, description scrolling off / slow / normal |
| **Content Display** | Hiding unwatched episodes' details (spoilers), logo or text titles, the poster on details pages |
| **Preview** | Samples of buttons, the library, details and the player; compare with what's applied now |
| **Saved Custom Themes** | Three slots to save, load and rename; replacing a used slot asks first |
| **Apply & Recovery** | Apply, discard, go back to what was applied, or reset the theme |

**Button shapes:** theme default, square, slightly rounded, rounded, pill and angled corners. Buttons' backgrounds, outlines and shadows follow the shape; posters and avatars keep theirs.

**Background colours:** theme default, black, dark, light, midnight blue, ocean blue, deep teal, forest green, royal purple, plum, burgundy, warm brown and slate blue.

## Saving

Changes are a draft until you **apply** them; **Options** applies. Leaving with unsaved changes asks whether to apply, discard or keep editing. Your custom slots and their names are saved when you apply. Reset only touches the appearance, never accounts, sources or playback settings.

## Reading and spoilers

- **□** shows a full description, even with scrolling turned off.
- On a hidden episode, **□** first reveals it, without marking it watched.
- Reduced motion stops descriptions from scrolling.
- When the server has no logo for a title, its name is shown as text.

The appearance only changes Emby5: not your server's web themes or other apps. Dimming the background doesn't change the video's brightness or HDR, and the text size doesn't change subtitles.
