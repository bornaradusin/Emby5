Emby5 builds on the work of many people in the PS5 homebrew and open-source communities. Full credit for their projects belongs to their developers and contributors.

| Project | |
| --- | --- |
| [Jelly5](https://github.com/02dnot/Jelly5) by **02dnot** | The original idea and work |
| [Nuvio PS5](https://github.com/theghostonline/Nuvio-PS5) by **Husam Osman / theghostonline** | The PS5 application and playback foundations |
| [EVO Player PS5](https://github.com/sainsaji/EVO-PLAYER-PS5) by **sainsaji** | Native PS5 media playback, decoding, rendering and the media engine |
| [ProsperoTV](https://github.com/blackbearreloaded/ProsperoTV) by **BlackBearReloaded** | Reference and inspiration for Live TV (IPTV) |
| [PS5 Homebrew UI](https://github.com/blackbearreloaded/ps5-homebrew-ui) by **BlackBearReloaded** | The designs behind 30 of the themes, adapted to Emby5's renderer; full credit for the original designs is theirs |
| [PS5 Payload SDK](https://github.com/ps5-payload-dev/sdk) by **ps5-payload-dev**, and **PacBrew** | The SDK, toolchain and packages |
