Emby5 is a native Emby and Jellyfin client for a PS5 that runs homebrew. It browses your libraries, plays your films, shows and music with the PS5's own video decoder, and adds Live TV, video on demand and Seerr requests. The app's title ID is `PPSA99515` and its tile on the home screen is called **Emby5**. This wiki describes Emby5 **1.2.9**.

![The Emby5 home screen](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bornaradusin/Emby5/main/docs/Home.jpg)

Emby5 contains no media, channels or subscriptions. It plays what your own server and providers give you.

## What Emby5 does

- **Connects to Emby and Jellyfin**, found on your network or by address, with several saved users and servers.
- **Browses your libraries**: Home rows, films, shows, seasons and episodes, music, sorting, filters, A–Z and search.
- **Plays natively**: H.264 and HEVC (including 4K and Main 10) on the PS5's hardware decoder, HDR10 and HLG, AV1 in software, Direct Play, Direct Stream or the server's transcode.
- **Handles audio and subtitles**: track choice, subtitle search, styling and delay, and optional HDMI bitstream for AC-3, E-AC-3 and DTS core.
- **Skips intros and recaps**, plays the next episode, and can ask "Are you still watching?".
- **Plays music** with a mini player, queue, shuffle, repeat and lyrics.
- **Shows Live TV** from your server, Xtream Codes providers and M3U/M3U8 playlists, and **video on demand** from Xtream catalogues.
- **Discovers and requests** films and shows through Seerr, if you have it.
- **Looks the way you like**: 32 themes and detailed appearance settings.

## What you need

- A PS5 that can run homebrew apps.
- An Emby or Jellyfin server you can reach from the console, or an IPTV provider you're entitled to use.
