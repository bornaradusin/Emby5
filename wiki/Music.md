Emby5 plays music from Emby and Jellyfin.

- Browse **artists, albums and playlists**.
- Music keeps playing while you move around the app, with a **mini player**. The **touchpad** opens **Now Playing** while music plays.
- The **queue**: see what's next, play from any track, **shuffle**, and **repeat** one or all.
- **Lyrics**, when your server has them.
- Instant **mixes** from an artist, album or track, when the server offers one.

Album covers load in the background. When an album has no cover, a track's picture is used if it has one; otherwise a placeholder stays.
