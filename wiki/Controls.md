## Live TV

In the Live TV tab, **Left / Right** move along a group's channels and **Up / Down** between groups.

In full-screen Live TV with the controls hidden, **Left / Right** play the previous or next channel in the group, and **Up / Down** the first channel of the previous or next group. One press is one switch. **Options** opens the controls, **○** hides them, and while they're visible the D-pad works as usual. See [Live TV](Live-TV#switching-channels-while-watching).

## Search

**△** opens or edits the search for the section you're in: the selected library, the selected Music view, Seerr in Discover, the loaded Live TV channels, or the selected VOD type. **○** clears or closes it and takes you back.

Some buttons do more while you manage your own Live TV groups; the screen shows which.

Emby5 also uses the DualSense's light bar, and HDMI Device Link (HDMI-CEC) buttons on the TV's remote where the TV and console support them.
