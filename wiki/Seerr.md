If you run Seerr, Emby5 can discover and request films and shows through it. Without it, the **Discover** tab doesn't appear.

## Setting up

In **Settings › Discover/Seerr**:

1. Enter Seerr's address. Use its local address, the one the PS5 can reach.
2. Choose how to sign in: with your Emby password, a Seerr account (email and password), or automatically through Quick Connect.
3. **Test connection** shows whether Seerr answers and who you're signed in as.

Seerr gets everything from TMDB itself: the PS5 only talks to your server and to Seerr. The Seerr settings are stored on the console.

## Discover

- **Trending**, **popular** and **upcoming** films and shows, and Seerr search with **△**.
- **My requests** and the status of each title: available, partly available, requested, pending approval, declined.
- **View in library** opens a title your server already has.

## Requesting

- Request a **film**, or a **series** with the seasons you choose (all seasons, all missing seasons, or some).
- Choose a quality profile and root folder (Radarr / Sonarr) when your account is allowed to.
- Your request quota is shown when there is one.
- **Request more seasons** later, and **withdraw** a request (press twice).

What you can do depends on your Seerr version, how it's set up and your account's permissions. A request may need an administrator's approval.
